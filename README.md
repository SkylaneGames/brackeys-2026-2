## Theme 

Trust No One

# Trust Vector

Forced into an asteroid storm you, the captain, must use all of the tools at your disposal to reach safety before the time runs out and your ship is consumed by the asteroid field.

Luckily for you your ship is equipped with two of the latest and "greatest" AI navigation systems which will pilot the ship through the field with haste. Unluckily for you, they can't agree on the safest path through the field and it's up to you to decide which you should trust for any given maneuver.
